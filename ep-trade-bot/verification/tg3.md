## 6. Deliberate differences from OpenAlgo

- **Unknown symbol.** OpenAlgo sends `tradingsymbol: "None"` to Kite when a symbol is not in the master contract (recorded in fixture `placeorder_unknown_symbol`). EP Trade bot refuses before sending anything.
- **API key in the header.** OpenAlgo's /funds schema requires `apikey` in the body, so its header is refused there (fixture `auth_header_key`). EP Trade bot accepts `X-API-KEY` on every endpoint, as the brief asks.
- **Analyzer mode is per account.** OpenAlgo has one platform-wide switch. Here the API key names the account and the account is PAPER or LIVE, so `/analyzer` reports the account's mode and `/analyzer/toggle` explains this instead of switching. A platform-wide switch that diverts live exits to the sandbox is exactly what the CLAUDE.md invariant warns about.
- **Guard and risk on API orders.** OpenAlgo has no per-account risk limits on /api/v1. Here API orders pass the environment guard and the account limits like every other order; a refusal is a 400 (403 for the environment lock).
- **Modify on a sandbox order** is refused with a plain message (cancel and place again); the sandbox has no modify yet.
- **No server-side tick throttling.** The plan said the proxy throttles. OpenAlgo removed that (it dropped ticks in bursts); the port follows OpenAlgo: every tick is forwarded, and a slow client's queue is bounded and drops its oldest messages.

**Deliberate differences.** Every one is recorded from the Python plugin, then the
fixture is changed to EP Trade bot's answer and compared in full, broker traffic
included (`tests/EPTradeBot.Contract.Tests/DhanContractTests.cs`).

- **The app key leaves the server on a modify.** OpenAlgo sends the whole `BROKER_API_KEY` value (`client_id:::app_key`) to Dhan as `dhanClientId` on every modify. EP Trade bot sends the client id. Recorded in all three modify fixtures.
- **A refused cancel is reported as cancelled** (both plugins accept any reply). EP Trade bot reports Dhan's refusal.
- **A refused modify answers "internal error"** (Dhan only). EP Trade bot passes Dhan's reason on.
- **Close all reports success with an exit refused.** EP Trade bot names the position still open, as it already did for Zerodha.
- **Funds show zero cash when Dhan refuses the token.** EP Trade bot says the token has expired; a zero is never shown as a balance.
- **Quotes answer success with every price 0** when Dhan's market data is not subscribed. EP Trade bot answers with the reason.
- **The sandbox plugin invents prices.** Executed: in the committed fixture, candles whose last close is 805.90 are reported as an LTP of 806.72, and with no candles at all it reports 100.51 with open interest 87937; open interest always comes from a hash of the security id. The noise is seeded by the minute, so each recording shows different numbers (an earlier one gave 804.49 and 99.13). EP Trade bot reports the candles as they are and answers "no price" when there are none.
- **A part-traded sandbox order survives cancel-all**, although the order book shows it open and its remainder can still fill. EP Trade bot cancels it.
- **Unknown symbol:** OpenAlgo sends `securityId: null`; EP Trade bot refuses before sending.
- **Wording only:** a refused order carries Dhan's reason instead of "Failed to place order", and a stop-loss-market order on an instrument with no tick size says why instead of "internal error" (same status 500, nothing sent).

**A risk gap closed.** Dhan allows one quote call a second, so a position's price
can come back as 0. The LIVE risk check valued such a position at 0, so the
position-value limit could be passed unchecked. A position with no price is now
valued at its average price
(`OpenAlgoApiTests.A_live_position_the_broker_cannot_price_still_counts_against_the_position_value_limit`).

## 12. Pass 5: symbol master, option symbols and multi-order endpoints

**What was built.** Nine /api/v1 endpoints, in three partial files of
`Infrastructure/OpenAlgoApi/OpenAlgoApiService`:

- **Symbol master** (`.Symbols.cs`): /symbol, /search, /expiry and GET /instruments (JSON or CSV). Each account reads its own broker's master contract; OpenAlgo held one broker's at a time.
- **Option symbols** (`.Options.cs`): /optionsymbol. Without `strike_int`, ATM is the listed strike nearest the underlying's last price and offsets walk the listed strikes. With it, ATM is the price rounded to the interval, half to even as Python's `round`. On MCX, CDS and the other exchanges with no spot, the price is taken from the nearest unexpired future of exactly that product (CRUDEOIL, never CRUDEOILM).
- **Several orders per request** (`.Composite.cs`): /basketorder, /splitorder, /optionsorder and /optionsmultiorder. BUY orders and legs go before SELL, as in OpenAlgo.

**Every part is its own order through the dispatcher.** The environment guard, the kill
switch and the account's risk limits apply to each order of a basket, each part of a split
and each leg, and each is audited. OpenAlgo's live basket and split called the broker
directly, past its own order service.
`OpenAlgoApiTests.Each_order_of_a_basket_and_each_part_of_a_split_passes_the_risk_check_on_its_own`:
with a 10-share limit, a basket of 50 and 5 sends only the 5. A split of 25 in tens sends
three orders. A split of 40 in twenties sends nothing. The Development test now also
sends all four new write endpoints and requires each to be refused with nothing sent.

**Supporting changes.**

- **Freeze limits are seeded.** EP Trade bot had never loaded NSE's F&O freeze limits, so every lookup answered 0 ("no limit known"). That affected more than these endpoints: the kill switch slices exits by these limits, and with none it would have sent a single exit above the exchange's limit, which the exchange refuses. The NFO rows of OpenAlgo's `data/qtyfreeze.csv` (214) are now loaded at startup into an empty table only, as OpenAlgo does, so a limit an admin edited is kept (`OpenAlgoApiTests.Freeze_limits_are_seeded_once_and_an_edited_limit_is_kept`).
